Apple's $1,999 Foldable Sells Out for Months Before the Coffee Gets Cold

Delivery estimates for the iPhone Duo slid to late January within an hour of preorders opening, though stores may still have some on October 23rd.

The wire said: iPhone Duo orders are already delayed into 2027 · The Verge

By The Daily Spin Desk · TechnologyOct 16, 2026 15:38 UTC · 1 min read

Catch up

Apple announced its first foldable iPhone, the Duo, in September, using the shorter passport-style shape Samsung introduced earlier this year. Preorders opened at 8AM ET on Friday, with a store launch set for October 23rd.

Why it matters

Anyone who wants a Duo this year may have to leave the house and stand in a store, an arrangement the smartphone industry had spent a decade trying to eliminate. Delays like this also signal either strong demand or thin supply, and Apple is not obliged to say which.

Apple opened preorders for the iPhone Duo at 8AM ET on Friday, and in less than an hour the delivery estimate had moved into next year. The Verge reported late January dates for every configuration on Apple's website. That is a remarkably long wait for a product that, as of breakfast, had not yet been sold to anyone.

The Duo starts at $1,999 for the 256GB model and comes in "star white" and "night sky." Translation: it costs about as much as a decent used car tire set, and it comes in two colors that sound like a candle collection. Apple announced it in September, and it uses the shorter passport-style design that Samsung introduced earlier this year, so Apple is entering a category it did not invent with the confidence of a company that rarely needs to.

Why the delay? The Verge offered three explanations: high demand, low supply on Apple's side, or a mix of both. Translation: either everyone wants one, Apple did not make many, or both, which is the traditional and most flattering outcome for a company. Apple has not, in the material we have seen, said which it is, and a shortage has never been known to hurt the brand's image.

There is a workaround. The foldable is slated to hit stores on October 23rd, and The Verge noted that Apple typically reserves some units for in-store availability. So online buyers face a January wait, while those willing to show up in person might walk out with a device sooner. After years of being told to order from the couch, customers are being gently reminded that the building still exists.

Whether the January date holds, slips further, or collapses as supply catches up is the thing to watch. Until then, the Duo remains available to everyone in theory and to almost no one on a deadline.
