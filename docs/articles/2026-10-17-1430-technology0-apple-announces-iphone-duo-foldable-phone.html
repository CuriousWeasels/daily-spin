Apple Unveils $1,999 Foldable iPhone Duo, Sells Out of 2026 in Under an Hour

Delivery estimates slipped into late January within an hour of preorders opening, which Apple described as demand that was 'nothing short of extraordinary'.

The wire said: All the news about the iPhone Duo · The Verge

By The Daily Spin Desk · TechnologyOct 17, 2026 14:30 UTC · 2 min read

Catch up

Apple announced its first foldable phone, the iPhone Duo, at its 'Surprise and shine' event, years after Samsung, Google, Huawei and Xiaomi shipped their own. Preorders opened at 8AM ET on Friday, October 16, with in-store sales slated for October 23.

Why it matters

A $1,999 starting price makes the Duo a test of how much buyers will pay for a new form factor, and repair costs of up to $1,499 raise the price of dropping it. Online buyers may wait until 2027, though Apple typically holds some units for stores.

Apple has announced the iPhone Duo, its first foldable phone, and according to The Verge, delivery estimates for every configuration slipped to late January in less than an hour after preorders opened. The device starts at $1,999 for the 256GB model. Apple called preorder demand 'nothing short of extraordinary', in a statement reported by Bloomberg. Translation: the phone is hard to get, which in consumer electronics is a feature that can be sold twice.

The hardware, as described by The Verge, is a 5.4-inch outer display that unfolds to a 7.6-inch inner screen with a matte 'nano-texture' finish and a barely visible crease. It has a main camera and a wide-angle camera, an A20 Pro chip shared with the iPhone 18 Pro, and an IP68 rating, meaning it is built to survive dust and water. Face ID is gone, replaced by Touch ID on the power button. Apple Pencil support is promised later this year.

The Verge's reviewers noted the company arrived late. Huawei, Samsung and Xiaomi all shipped similar passport-shaped foldables first, which one writer blamed on Apple's slow-moving supply chain. The argument for waiting is software: Apple rebuilt iOS 27's controls and apps for the format, including moving the dock to the side. Apple has long argued that being last is acceptable provided one is also polished. Customers are now testing that theory with $1,999 each.

Apple's new CEO John Ternus and marketing chief Greg Joswiak also addressed copycats in interviews. The Verge's explanation was that secrecy is nearly impossible when Apple and its rivals share the same global suppliers. In other words, everyone builds the same phone in the same factories and then holds a press event to explain how different it is.

Then there is the bill for gravity. Without AppleCare Plus, Apple's published estimates run to $229 for a battery service, $999 for inner display damage and $1,499 for 'other damage'. Repairing the folding screen can therefore cost about as much as buying a new iPhone. AppleCare Plus is, in this arrangement, a second product sold alongside the first to manage how nervous the first one makes you.

Buyers who missed the early window have options. Apple said it will keep taking online orders, and The Verge noted the company typically holds back some units for stores, so queueing in person on October 23 may beat clicking for January. Apple did not say whether any inventory would be available to people without preorders. Waiting in line remains the only delivery method with no estimate.
